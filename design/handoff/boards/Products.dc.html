A music model we post-trained ourselves.

Murmur starts from an open-source music model. We post-trained about 90% of it on lofi and noise, so it knows rain from room tone and a tired piano from a bright one. You describe what you want to hear and it writes a seamless loop for sleep, focus, study or rest.

Fresh puzzles every morning, from our own model.

We trained a puzzle model in house. Every night it writes a new set of eleven, then checks each one: a single answer, fair clues, nothing repeated, easy to hard. What fails is thrown away, so you never get AI filler. Because the model is ours, a fresh set costs almost nothing to make.
