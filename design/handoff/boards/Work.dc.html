Sound you describe.
Looped all night long.

Murmur makes the sound for focus and sleep. Pick instruments, a mood and a texture, or shape a noise to the room you are in. It writes a loop that joins end to start without a seam, so it can play all night.

12instruments, from piano to harp
6textures: vinyl, rain, cafe, tape, wind, fire
1 to 4 minloops, played for hours

We took an open-source music model and rebuilt it for one job. About 90% of its weights are post-trained by us, on 14,000 hours of lofi and noise scored by ear for a single thing: nothing in it should pull your attention. It now writes a whole loop in 8 steps, and the last sample lands exactly where the first one began.

The puzzles people pay for,
free every morning.

Morrow has the kinds of daily puzzles people usually pay a subscription for: a crossword and a mini, five-letter words, bundles, word threads, number place and more. AI writes them, which makes them cheap enough to give away, so at midnight the same eleven arrive for everyone, free. Eight Odds games, where you play against a language model’s guesses, a Wednesday special and a monthly Almanac sit around them.

11new puzzles every day
1 answereach, proved by a solver
8 Oddsgames against a model

Our puzzle model is trained in house to do what a puzzle editor does. Every night it drafts about 4,000 candidates. Solvers then play every one: any puzzle with a second answer, a dead end or a dull path is thrown out, and the rest are ranked by how they feel to solve. Eleven survive. Because the model is small and ours, a whole day of fresh puzzles costs less than a cent to make. That is the point: AI should make good entertainment cheaper for everyone, not another subscription.

Client work · Regulatory model ESC

A compliance SLM,
private to the client’s cloud.

It reads one regulatory article and returns the risks and controls that bind, or nothing when the text does not. A reviewer still signs. Built for a client in a regulated industry, it runs inside their own AWS account.

A 27B specialist model, developed from an expert-labelled workflow and built with supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning. Synthetic data covers the rare labels. It is scored on a held-out gold set, with the expert labels withheld from training.

Client work · AI architecture ESC

Library-backed extraction.
Every answer is one you know.

Two retrieval paths run side by side: one reads the document, the other searches a library of risks and controls. Extraction is bound to that library, so every answer is a control the reviewers already recognise, or nothing at all.

2 pathsthe document and the library
Boundevery answer maps to the library
Any domainstandardized, or built with you

It works in standardized domains, where that library already exists, and in unstandardized ones, where we build the library with the client first and the same pipeline runs on it.

We design AI frameworks and architectures like this for other needs too: agent workflows with a person in the loop, document review, decision support. Each one is shaped around the job, and runs where the data already lives.
