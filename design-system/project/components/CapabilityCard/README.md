# CapabilityCard

A service card: the service's illustration on a plate, then title, two sentences and tags.

**Props**: `art` (an Illustration `kind`), `title`, `body` (two sentences), `tags` (three or four).

- Four on the home page in a 2×2 grid: Product engineering, Applied AI, Modernisation, Technical diligence. AWS has its own section.
- The body says what we do and how, in that order. No outcomes we cannot show.
