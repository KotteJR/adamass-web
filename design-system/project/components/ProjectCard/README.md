# ProjectCard

A card on the Work page. Our products show a real screenshot; client work shows an illustration and links to a short written page.

**Props**: `name`, `kind` (`product` default, or `client`), `year`, `oneLiner`, `src` / `srcDark` (products), `art` (clients: an Illustration `kind`), `tags`, `href`.

- Client cards never show the client's screens. Their pages are a few paragraphs on what they needed and where we helped.
- The one-liner says what the thing is, in under eight words.
