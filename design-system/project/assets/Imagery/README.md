# Imagery

Hero photographs: one scene, two times of day. Calm, mostly sky, a low band of pale limestone in fog.

- `hero-light.webp` — early morning, pale gray-blue sky. Light theme.
- `hero-dark.webp` — blue hour, navy to slate. Dark theme.

Both are 1920×1088, generated on our own machine with FLUX.2 [klein] 4B (Apache 2.0 licence). Keep the headline over the empty sky; product windows sit over the rocks.
