# Logos

The original brand files, unchanged. On the web, prefer the `Logo` component, which follows the theme.

- `adamass-lockup-primary.svg` — ink `#0B1B33` letters, blue `#0066FF` AB. On white and light grounds.
- `adamass-lockup-reversed.svg` — white letters, AB `#4D94FF`. On dark grounds.
- `adamass-lockup-deep.svg` — deep blue `#0047B3` letters, AB `#4D94FF`. On pale blue grounds.
- `adamass-lockup-knockout.svg` — white letters. On a blue `#0066FF` ground.
- `adamass-icon-knockout.svg` — white `[a]` on a blue `#0066FF` tile. Favicons, avatars.
- `adamass-icon-reversed.svg` — white `[a]` on a near-black `#050B16` tile.

These files use the previous blue `#0066FF`. The token `blue` is `#2B5CFF`.
